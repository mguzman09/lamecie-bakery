OUR STORY

More than just a bakery.

Lamecie Bakery is a dessert shop specializing in cheesecakes and offering a variety of classic and innovative flavors.

Our goal is to create a beautiful, cozy, and welcoming environment where customers can enjoy high-quality desserts and have a memorable experience.

Created by Lara Torres, Maciel Nassar & Martina Guzman.
