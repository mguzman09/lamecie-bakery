OUR STORY

More than just a bakery.

Lamecie Bakery is a premium dessert bakery located in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz. We specialize in high-quality cheesecakes and innovative desserts.

Our goal is to create a beautiful, cozy, and welcoming environment where customers can enjoy delicious desserts and have a memorable experience.

Created by Lara Torres, Maciel Nassar & Martina Guzman.
